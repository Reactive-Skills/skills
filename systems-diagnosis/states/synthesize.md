# SYNTHESIZE

Present a concise summary with the target behavior, confirmed boundary, system map, causal hypotheses, evidence, uncertainty, selected intervention, test plan, and open questions.

Keep observed facts separate from inferences.

State what evidence would change the diagnosis.

Store final_diagnosis in contextUpdates when the runtime supports it.

## Anti-Shortcut Gate

- Do not claim certainty beyond the evidence.
- Do not omit relevant delays, counterforces, or side effects.
- Do not imply that the user has approved or executed the test.

This is the terminal state.
