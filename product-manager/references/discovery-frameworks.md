# Product Discovery & Opportunity Validation Frameworks

Empirical discovery reference for evaluating product opportunities and validating market demand.

## 1. Discovery Interview Protocol (The Mom Test)
- **Anchor on Past Actions**: Ask users how they handled the problem last week, not how they might handle it next year.
- **Quantify the Friction**: Measure current workarounds in hours lost, dollars spent, or broken workflows.
- **Avoid Leading Pitches**: Never ask hypothetical validation questions ("Do you think our tool would help?"). Keep focus on the user's current environment.

## 2. Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Opportunity Canvas
Structure every requirement as an outcome-driven job statement:
- **Core Functional Job**: The baseline task the user seeks to accomplish.
- **Emotional Job**: How the user wants to feel (confident, in control, respected).
- **Social Job**: How the user wants to be perceived by their team or peers.
- **Outcome Metrics**: Quantifiable success criteria (e.g., "Reduce time to detect schema drift from 4 hours to under 30 seconds").

## 3. SMART Requirement Scoping
- **Specific**: Unambiguous domain model with bounded context.
- **Measurable**: Automated test, metric, or verifiable output artifact.
- **Achievable**: Implementable within the sprint / slice budget without speculative dependencies.
- **Relevant**: Directly serves the validated opportunity and customer problem.
- **Time-bound**: Explicit delivery and verification gates.
