# BLOCKED

## Objective

Report why the run cannot continue or meet its approved DoD, including a user requested stop.

## Required handoff

Name each unmet criterion, evidence, attempted repair, and smallest user decision or access needed.
Separate completed work from incomplete work.
Keep the approved DoD and its current version visible.
Do not label blocked work complete.

## Stop

Record the blocker and wait for user direction.

## Atomic Gate

- Stopped or unmet DoD items are identified.
- Available evidence and attempted repairs are recorded.
- The smallest required user decision or access request is clear.
- No incomplete work is reported as complete.
