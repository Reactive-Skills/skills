# Experimental design

Clarify the intervention or manipulation, comparison condition, assignment method, outcomes, measures, procedure, and timing.
Ask whether random assignment is feasible and ethically acceptable.
Match the design to the causal question and state threats to validity.
Do not imply randomization, control, or causal certainty unless the plan supports it.
Continue to analysis and validity, clarification, or synthesis.

## Signals
Emit `CONTINUE_TO_ANALYSIS`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Name the comparison and assignment plan.
- [ ] Surface ethical and practical constraints.
