# Survey design

Clarify whether the survey describes a population or examines relationships among variables.
Ask about the target population, sampling frame, recruitment, instrument, administration, response burden, and timing as needed.
Address construct definition, item quality, reliability, validity, missing responses, and the intended analysis.
Do not invent sample sizes or claim representativeness.
Continue to analysis and validity, clarification, or synthesis.

## Signals
Emit `CONTINUE_TO_ANALYSIS`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Distinguish target population from accessible sample.
- [ ] Mark instrument evidence and sampling limits.
