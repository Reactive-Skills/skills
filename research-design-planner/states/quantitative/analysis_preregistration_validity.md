# Analysis, preregistration, and validity

Connect each research question to variables, measures, and an analysis plan.
Separate confirmatory hypotheses from exploratory analyses.
Ask whether preregistration is planned and what decisions it will document.
Consider measurement quality, internal and external validity, reliability, missing data, and limits on interpretation.
Do not invent a sample-size calculation, statistical test, or result.
Return to the survey or experiment, ask for clarification, or synthesize.

## Signals
Emit `RETURN_TO_SURVEY`, `RETURN_TO_EXPERIMENT`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Tie analysis to questions and measures.
- [ ] Mark assumptions and unknowns.
