# Quantitative path

Use the selected survey or experiment path.
Then align sampling, measures, analysis, preregistration where applicable, validity, reliability, and interpretation.
Do not request every detail before helping with the user's current question.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Match measures and analysis to the research questions.
- [ ] Keep feasibility and causal limits visible.
