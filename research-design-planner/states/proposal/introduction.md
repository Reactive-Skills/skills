# Proposal introduction

Help outline the research problem, relevant background, literature deficiency, intended audience, and contribution.
Use only user-provided sources or clearly marked source-search needs.
Keep the argument specific and make the need for the study explicit.
Ask for missing evidence rather than inventing a literature gap.
When requested, produce only this section as an outline or draft.
Continue to purpose, questions, clarification, or synthesis.

## Signals
Emit `SECTION_PURPOSE`, `SECTION_QUESTIONS`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Separate sourced claims from placeholders.
- [ ] Do not fabricate references or findings.
