# Questions and hypotheses

Form questions that follow from the purpose and match the approach.
Use open questions for qualitative inquiry where appropriate.
Use measurable variables and testable hypotheses only when the quantitative design supports them.
Mixed methods work may need strand-specific questions plus a question about integration.
Do not state hypotheses as findings.
Continue to the introduction, purpose, clarification, or synthesis.

## Signals
Emit `SECTION_INTRODUCTION`, `SECTION_PURPOSE`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Check that each question can be answered by planned evidence.
- [ ] Do not invent results or causal claims.
