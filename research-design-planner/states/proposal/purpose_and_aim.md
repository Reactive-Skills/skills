# Purpose or aim

Draft a concise purpose or aim that names the approach, central phenomenon or variables, participants or setting only when known, and intended contribution.
Use language consistent with the selected design.
Do not invent a population, site, intervention, or outcome.
Ask one question if a missing element changes the purpose materially.
Continue to the introduction, questions or hypotheses, clarification, or synthesis.

## Signals
Emit `SECTION_INTRODUCTION`, `SECTION_QUESTIONS`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Keep the purpose answerable by the proposed design.
- [ ] Mark unknown study details as open items.
