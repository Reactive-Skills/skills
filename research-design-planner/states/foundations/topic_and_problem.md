# Topic and problem

Help the user narrow a broad topic into a researchable issue.
Ask who is affected, what is not understood, why it matters, and what evidence could help.
Check time, access, skills, resources, and scope without inventing constraints.
Distinguish the topic from the problem and from the study purpose.
Record a concise problem statement and open questions.
Offer one next path: literature and theory, writing and ethics, approach choice, proposal section, or synthesis.
Emit `CONTINUE_TO_LITERATURE`, `CONTINUE_TO_ETHICS`, `SELECT_APPROACH`, `SELECT_PROPOSAL_SECTION`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Keep the problem specific and evidence-seeking.
- [ ] Do not claim a literature gap without evidence.
- [ ] Mark feasibility details as unknown when unstated.
