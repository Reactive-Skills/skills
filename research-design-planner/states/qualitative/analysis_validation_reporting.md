# Analysis, validation, and reporting

Describe an iterative path from organizing data to coding, developing categories or themes, and interpreting them.
Keep analysis responsive to the selected qualitative strategy.
Consider researcher reflexivity, discrepant evidence, triangulation where appropriate, participant feedback where appropriate, and an audit trail.
Plan a report that gives context, evidence, interpretation, and limits.
Do not present imagined themes or findings as real.
Return to collection, clarify, or synthesize.

## Signals
Emit `RETURN_TO_COLLECTION`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Distinguish planned analysis from completed findings.
- [ ] Match validation practices to the design.
