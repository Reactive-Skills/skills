# Access, sampling, and collection

Use the selected strategy: narrative, phenomenology, ethnography, case study, or grounded theory.
Clarify access, setting, unit of analysis, researcher role, relationships, reflexivity, and positionality.
Plan a purposeful sampling logic and suitable data sources such as interviews, observations, documents, or artifacts.
Consider consent, privacy, data stewardship, and participant burden.
Do not invent access, participants, or sample size.
Continue to analysis and reporting, clarify, or synthesize.

## Signals
Emit `CONTINUE_TO_ANALYSIS`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.
