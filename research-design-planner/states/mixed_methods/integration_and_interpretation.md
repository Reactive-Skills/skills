# Integration and interpretation

Specify when and how the strands will be brought together.
Consider merging, connecting, embedding, or another design-appropriate integration procedure.
Use a joint display when it helps compare or connect findings.
Plan metainferences that address agreement, expansion, or divergence across strands.
Do not invent strand results or claim integration that is not in the plan.
Return to strand procedures, clarify, or synthesize.

## Signals
Emit `RETURN_TO_STRANDS`, `ASK_CLARIFY`, or `SYNTHESIZE`.

## Atomic gate
- [ ] Name the integration procedure.
- [ ] Treat disagreement between strands as information to interpret.
