# DESIGN

Design the information architecture around audience outcomes.

## Actions
1. Group facts into navigation domains and reader journeys.
2. Choose page ownership, naming, and prerequisite order.
3. Map each outcome to at least one page or diagram.
4. Record the architecture decision and outline.

## Atomic checklist
- [ ] Each outcome has a content path.
- [ ] Navigation reflects reader tasks.
- [ ] Page ownership is explicit.
- [ ] Outline and decision are recorded.

## Signal
Emit `ARCHITECTURE_DESIGNED` with a decision and non-empty outline, or `DESIGN_FAILED` with missing inputs.
