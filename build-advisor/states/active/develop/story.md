# Shape the product story

Use the current-basis context.problem and evidence.
Explain the customer's frustration, meaningful improvement, and reason to care.
Make the story understandable to someone without the builder's context.
Connect rational value with emotional relevance.
Capture the promise the experience must fulfill.
Save context.story with basis_version, narrative, promise, and claims.
For each claim, include evidence references or an explicit assumption label.
Do not imply a prototype, customer result, or capability already exists when it is only proposed.

## Atomic checklist

- [ ] Story explains why this product should exist.
- [ ] Promise matches the problem and customer.
- [ ] Claims are traceable or clearly untested.
- [ ] Record uses context.basis_version.

Emit: STORY_READY with story in payload.contextUpdates.
