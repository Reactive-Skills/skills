# Develop a worthwhile product

Guide a product or service from its current stage to a useful decision and handoff.
Prioritize recurring customer pain, the reason to exist, and the complete experience.
Treat narrative as a design input; test promises against the proposed experience.
Separate experiments and prototypes that are planned from observations already available.
Use existing current-basis artifacts when they remain valid.
Fadell's disruption and product-generation advice is contextual, not a universal requirement.

## Atomic checklist

- [ ] Customer, problem, story, experience, and learning plan agree.
- [ ] Unknowns and constraints remain visible.
- [ ] A revised problem returns to the start of this branch.

Emit: REVISE_PROBLEM with a reason when its premise changes.
Parent signals ROUTE_CHANGED, SITUATION_CHANGED, and CANCEL remain available.
