# Inspect the proposal

Identify the actual artifact or proposal in context.situation, prior route artifacts, or user-provided material.
Ask for missing material needed to review it.
Examine customer pain, why it should exist, promised value, touchpoints, timing, resources, and incentives.
Use context.evidence to distinguish observed contradictions from suspected weaknesses.
For each finding, record its evidence references, consequence, and what would resolve it.
No findings is valid when the scoped review supports that result.
Save context.assessment with basis_version, artifact, summary, and findings.

## Atomic checklist

- [ ] Artifact and review scope are explicit.
- [ ] Findings follow from inspected material.
- [ ] Unknowns are not presented as established defects.
- [ ] Record uses context.basis_version.

Emit: REVIEW_SCOPED with assessment in payload.contextUpdates.
