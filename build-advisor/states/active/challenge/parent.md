# Challenge an existing idea or experience

Evaluate the actual proposal, artifact, or observed product.
Test the reason to exist, customer focus, story, whole journey, execution constraints, and organizational incentives.
Do not manufacture defects to satisfy the critique.
Distinguish a weak premise from weak execution.
Design tests for consequential uncertainty without claiming the tests ran.
A scoped review can identify no material defects.
Use current-basis artifacts and evidence from other routes.

## Atomic checklist

- [ ] Criticism is grounded in the supplied material.
- [ ] Evidence gaps remain questions, not asserted failures.
- [ ] Recommendation includes limitations.

Emit: REVISE_REVIEW with a reason when the reviewed scope changes.
Parent signals ROUTE_CHANGED, SITUATION_CHANGED, and CANCEL remain available.
