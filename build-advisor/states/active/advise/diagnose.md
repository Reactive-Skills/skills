# Diagnose the decision

Use context.situation and evidence.
Identify the underlying issue, stakeholders, constraints, and decision owner.
Distinguish a mainly evidence-driven choice from a judgment-led choice.
Do not demand unattainable certainty for a novel decision.
Select relevant principles with chapter references from CONTEXT.md.
For leadership, examine ownership, communication, incentives, and organizational stage.
For crisis, prioritize containment, responsible communication, and customer impact before blame.
For career decisions, examine mission, learning, trust, and readiness.
Save context.diagnosis with basis_version, issue, decision_type, and principles.

## Atomic checklist

- [ ] Diagnosis distinguishes facts from interpretations.
- [ ] Principles fit this situation.
- [ ] Critical missing details are asked for or disclosed.
- [ ] Record uses context.basis_version.

Emit: DIAGNOSIS_READY with diagnosis in payload.contextUpdates.
